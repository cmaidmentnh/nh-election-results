Good to know

Do I need any experience?

No. If you can take a photo with your phone, you can do this. We email you simple instructions before Election Day.

What if my town has several voting machines?

Photograph every tape or results sheet that's posted, one photo each, and send them all together. We add them up.

What if results come in late?

Small towns often post within an hour. Bigger towns and hand-count towns can take longer. Send them whenever they're posted.

Can I cover more than one place?

Yes, up to {{ max_places }}, if you can get to both before results are posted.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do, and have them sign up too. If one of you can't make it, the other can still send results.
