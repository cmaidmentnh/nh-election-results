Report results from your polling place on Election Night

On {{ election_day }}, results get posted at every polling place in New Hampshire after the polls close. We need one person at each to snap a photo and send it to us, so we know where every race stands, town by town, as the night goes on.

{{ open_count }} of {{ places|length }} polling places still need a volunteer
What you'll do
  1. Be at your polling place when the polls close at 7:00 p.m.
  2. Wait for the moderator to announce and post the results.
  3. Take a photo of the posted results and send it from your phone using the personal link we email you. It takes about a minute.

Your own town, or one on your way home, is perfect. We'll confirm your spot by email.
