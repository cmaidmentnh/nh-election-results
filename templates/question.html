How to read it. Yes % is yes votes as a share of yes plus no. Green is at or above {% endraw %}{{ threshold }}{% raw %}%, red is below it. Dark colors mean every polling place in the town or county has reported; light colors mean some are still out. Gray means nothing is in yet. The statewide total is what decides it; town colors show where the support came from.

Method. Results come from the election-night returns (elections.nhhouse.gop), with city wards added together, and reload every minute. The returns are unofficial until the Secretary of State certifies them.
