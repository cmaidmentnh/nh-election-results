How to read it. Each arrow starts at a town. It points left and is blue when the Democratic margin grew compared with the earlier election, and right and red when the Republican margin grew. Longer arrows mean bigger shifts. Only towns with every polling place reported get an arrow; the rest are shaded lighter. "Shift, towns in" compares the same towns in both years, so it shows the direction of the night, not the final statewide margin.

Method. Margin is the Democratic share minus the Republican share of all votes cast in the race, with city wards added together. Election-night returns from elections.nhhouse.gop reload every minute and are unofficial until the Secretary of State certifies them. For Congress, each town is compared with its own district's race.
